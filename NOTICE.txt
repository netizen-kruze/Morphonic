Downloaded at the user's request, never bundled
-----------------------------------------------
- ContentVec content encoder — MIT (auspicious3000/contentvec; Transformers
  checkpoint by lengyue233, content-vec-best; the app downloads that
  checkpoint and assembles the ONNX model itself)
- RMVPE pitch model — MIT (yxlllc/RMVPE; ONNX export published by the RVC
  project, lj1995/VoiceConversionWebUI)
- Sample voice — MIT (RVC v2 pretrained generator f0G40k by the RVC project,
  lj1995/VoiceConversionWebUI; the app downloads that checkpoint and
  assembles the ONNX model itself)
- GPU acceleration, Windows — ONNX Runtime DirectML build (MIT, Microsoft)
  and DirectML (Microsoft DirectML license; its text is saved beside the
  library as DIRECTML-LICENSE.txt)
- GPU acceleration, Linux — ONNX Runtime CUDA build (MIT, Microsoft) and the
  NVIDIA CUDA 12 runtime, cuBLAS, cuFFT, cuRAND, cuDNN 9 and nvJitLink
  (NVIDIA Software License Agreements; their texts are saved beside the
  libraries as NVIDIA-CUDA-LICENSE.txt and NVIDIA-CUDNN-LICENSE.txt)

The optional .pth converter (tools/morphonic_export.py) uses PyTorch (BSD-3),
ONNX (Apache-2.0), onnxruntime (MIT) and Transformers (Apache-2.0), which
the user installs with pip.

A machine-readable manifest of installed models and their licenses is
written next to the models themselves (models_manifest.json, LICENSES.txt).

Full license texts for the bundled components: THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md.
